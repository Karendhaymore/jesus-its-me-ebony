Jesus, It's Me, Ebony

Being eleven is enough. ♡

Ebony Reed is eleven, and life suddenly has a lot more questions than answers.

A new city. A new school. New friends. Boys. Family. Faith. Growing up.

When life gets confusing, Ebony talks to the one person she believes is always listening, Jesus.

Funny, heartfelt, messy, and honest, Jesus, It's Me, Ebony is a coming-of-age story about finding your voice, questioning what you thought you knew, and discovering that being exactly who you are is enough.
